---
title: Mittens can wear your pictures now
tags: Developments, Done
---
His profile picture already changes itself for Halloween, Christmas, Easter, Ramadan and Eid, Pride and summer. Those are worked out from the date, so they simply happen.

Now you can add your own. **Mittens the Menace → Avatar → Add an avatar**: give it a name and the dates it should be up, and he will ask you to post the picture in the channel. It goes up on the first day and comes off on the last, on its own.

If the dates clash with something already booked — one of the built-in occasions, or another picture somebody added — he tells you exactly what it clashes with and on which days, and asks you to pick again. Two pictures fighting over the same day would just quietly pick one and lose the other, so he does not allow it.

Also in that drawer: what is up right now and what is coming next, pinning a picture by hand, handing it back to the calendar, and removing anything that was added. The built-in occasions stay where they are.
