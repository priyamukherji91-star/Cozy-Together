---
title: You can edit the things Mittens says
tags: Developments, Done
---
Mittens picks a lot of what he says at random — his Discord status, the daily and weekly reset announcements, what he says when somebody gets timed out, the pet feeding chatter, the footers on the wall of shame. All of it lived in the code, which meant every new line was a favour somebody had to ask for.

It is editable now, from the panel: **Mittens the Menace → Mittens custom lines**. Pick a set, read through it, add a line, change one, delete one. The arrows page through the long ones — there are 97 statuses in there.

Two safety nets, because this is his mouth:

• **Reset** puts the original lines back, one set at a time, whenever you want. Nothing you do here is permanent.
• He refuses lines that would break. Some lines have slots in them — the pet's name, how long a timeout is, who it was about. Write one that leaves out a slot it needs, or invents one that does not exist, and he tells you which and why rather than accepting it and then going quiet at nine in the evening.

Edits go live immediately and survive updates. The originals are always still there underneath.
