title: Smaller things that came with the panel
tags: Developments, Done

A handful of changes that are easy to miss on their own:

• **Birthdays** — you can add one for somebody else now, not only remove it. Useful for a typo, or for whoever never got round to setting theirs.

• **The way in** — the landing-zone gate and the get-roles menus repost together from one button, each into its own channel, and the ones they replace are cleared out instead of stacking up.

• **Purge** can clear a different channel than the one you are standing in.

• **Status ideas** come back to you privately now, in a block you can copy straight into the lines editor, instead of being posted where everyone can watch you shop for jokes.

• **Treats top-up** is open to staff rather than the owner alone — it only ever refills your own allowance, so there was never much to guard.

• **Mittens checks his own buttons** when he starts up. If something changes underneath one of them, he says so on the panel instead of waiting for somebody to press it and get a shrug.
